Created on June 19, 2025

2025   ·   random   journal

I have just finished first 5 blog drafts for AIO2025. I know I’m way behind schedule and there are still many lessons awaiting for me to write about. But I’ve been trying and learning how to manage it better throughout this process, so I’d like to celebrate this moment for not being a total bum.

I plan to take this weekend, especially tomorrow and the next day to work on this week’s lectures and homework. Hopefully, I’ll be done with them by Sunday, so I can start go back and add missing information as well modify what’s already there. Then editing comes whenever I have everything in one place.

I can’t believe how much I forgot what I was taught after each session and what I typed up. The amount was enormous! It still is if I must be honest. But listening to the recording and trying code out on my own makes a difference. One tiny difference every day compounds to a giant difference of the future. (I kinda take this sentence from Atomic Habits by James Clear. I’ll quote it sometime later.)

In addition to AI related stuff, I have learned more about using Markdown.

  • Make pretty and standard HTML tables with CSS.
  • Make colorful text.
  • Make table of contents appear.

Uhm, about English, I think I have been reusing same words a lot, so I have not learned much. But I know what I want to learn. It’s how to paraphrase sentences so they are meaningful, natural, and simple. Hopefully, reading more would help with it.

Last time, I said I learned using emojis better because of a post. Apparently, I didn’t. I myself feel overwhelmed looking at my posts with emojis everywhere. ChatGPT suggested I reduce their quantity. So yeah I think I will for now. I did for my lastest post and modified my first post. I think they look pretty good without so many emojis. I’ll take some others’ opinions when I’m ready later on; to be frank, I don’t quite trust ChatGPT even though I have used it often.

Anyway, it’s time for bed. I’m trying to make an effort to enforce a good habit of sleeping by 11pm here. Stay healthy and stay strong! All of us.
